# 02 · 竞品档案: Between 遗产与 LDR 情侣 App 全景

> 数据源: 帖05/07/10 全量评论 + 帖08 + 前次 App Store 实测(freesight)

## 一、Between: 从品类之王到信任破产的全过程

**基本盘**: 累计 3500 万下载的韩国"国民情侣App", 运营权 2025 年内两次转手(VCNC→Thingsflow→DLT Partners)。

**2025年9月事故**(帖05, 一手用户帖): 例行服务器维护中, 所有使用超 3 个月的免费用户的照片视频相册被永久删除且不可恢复。

用户原话三层结构:
- 使用定位: "it had become the **storage place** for all of our most precious photos and videos, with the assumption of **safe, private, and unlimited storage**"
- 伤害形态: "my girlfriend literally lost all the photos since it was her **only source of backup**"; "it's not just about the photos but **the memories we documented the way it was in the app**"(相册结构+评论也是记忆的一部分)
- 追责与要求: "**at least give us a window of time to make backups**"; "Imagine if YouTube, Facebook or WhatsApp deleted all your content"; "If there are at least responsive **I will think of buying**"

**事件后的用户流向**(同帖实测):
1. 数据分层托管——"continue to use Between for daily communication, but important dates we'll find other solution, **Google probably first due to their stability**"
2. 物理化回潮——"this has finally motivated us to create **physical photo albums**"
3. 替代品求购——"has anyone found an alternative to Between yet for photo sharing? I loved the feature where it **automatically sorted photos by date** 🥲"
4. 品牌惯性残留——两个月后的推荐帖里仍有人无脑推荐"Use Between"

## 二、LDR 情侣 App 全景: 一个帖 30+ 品牌的碎片化战场

帖07(85评)与帖10(216评可见54条)合计出现 **40+ 独立品牌**, 按定位归类:

| 定位 | 品牌(用户提及) | 用户评价原话 |
|---|---|---|
| 日常连接+游戏 | Cozy Couples, Bloom Together, Lovlee, SumOne, noteit, Orbs | "it gives us stuff to talk about"; bloom的"theme/doodles/widgets/Music generation"被赞"having fun since the morning" |
| 问答/治疗向 | Paired, Agapé, Lovefix, Couple's Mirror, Heart2Heart, Gottman Card Decks | "questions prompt arguments rather than dialogue"; "closer to therapy than play" |
| 照片/记忆 | Between, Couple Memory Book, Entwine, Keepsake类 | "automatically sorted photos by date"是点名怀念的功能 |
| Widget/便签 | LoveWidgets, drawn love notes, noteit, sharedmood | "he sends me cute doodles on my home screen when I wake up" |
| 任务/生活管理 | DailyQuest, Fenn, Amorno, Entwine, Hey Love | DailyQuest"almost every day"是罕见的高留存样本 |
| 硬件向 | Lovense | ×2 提及(亲密硬件) |
| 视频/游戏 | funcalling, duogames.org | 见05报告的体验破碎分析 |

**被验证的失败模式**(真实用户判词, 帖10):
- "Most of them felt either **too cute, too gamified, or like something you have to actively maintain** — which is usually the problem in the first place"
- "most felt **gimmicky or just died off after a week** because there wasn't really anything pulling you back into them"
- "They can be fun, but they only really help if **both people actually engage**"
- Paired 被两方独立吐槽: "feels kinda childish" + "questions prompt arguments"

**被验证的成功样本**:
- DailyQuest(todo 互相派任务+奖励): 挂靠既有生活习惯而非制造新义务
- Paired 正面付费案例: "$40 for an entire year and totally worth it"(新情侣主动了解彼此场景)
- Bloom Together 的胜出路径: 主题自定义+doodle+widget+音乐生成——**审美差异化在过剩市场里就是产品力**

## 三、供给侧的结构性观察

1. **开发者自推污染**: 帖07 里同一开发者同帖 4 次推自家 Pebbles; 帖10 里 54 条可见评论约 20 条自推, 版规删了 160+ 条。自然推荐位稀缺——**付费投放与 SEO 之外, "被真实用户自发推荐"是本赛道最稀缺的资产**。
2. **免费墙是集体软肋**: "all the apps i've seen so far are like that"(免费版残废)——付费转化差的上游原因。
3. **跨平台是基础门槛**: "I have IOS and my gf has android"——Web 产品天然通吃。
4. ** lifetime 送码刷榜**(r/AppGiveaway 多帖): Cutest Couple $99.99→FREE 等——说明独立供给的获客绝望程度。
5. **你的品牌在 Reddit 零痕迹**: yourlovepage/lovepage 系品牌词搜索 22 条结果全部是字面噪声——**竞品在 Reddit 没有自来水也没有差评**, 这既是机会(空白)也是警讯(它们也没做起来社区)。
