## 正文
回顾情书的变迁: 过去是课堂上传递的涂鸦信/夹在外套口袋里的纸条; 现在是" somehow sound like Pablo Neruda and your therapist teamed up "的AI代写信. 在 dating 更流动开放的当下(尤其queer空间), 个性化比以往更重要. 核心之问: 你宁愿收到一封略显笨拙但真心的手写便签, 还是一封辞藻完美的AI情书? "I wonder if tech is starting to write our emotions for us instead of with us."(科技是在代替我们表达情感, 还是与我们一起表达)

## 全部评论
#1 [1] of course handwritten notes. even if its not the best handwriting, not the best grammar... it is still more genuine than anything written by ai. **letting ai write a letter thats supposed to tell your love is the most insincere thing you can do, and the saddest letter you can receive**
#2 [1] Handwritten notes is still the best one. AI and other modern types will never replace the human handwriting and sincerity.

## 分析要点
1. 核心概念区分: "write our emotions FOR us"(代替) vs "WITH us"(协作)——用户接受后者拒绝前者
2. AI全托管代写情书=品类雷区("最不真诚的事"+"能收到的最悲伤的信")
3. AI的正确位置: 起草/润色/提供结构灵感, 最终输出必须携带本人痕迹(手写体/个人语料/自定义修改)
