[2026-04-02|GamerArceus|7] The go-to free options are Inkscape and draw.io, both of which handle scientific diagrams reasonably well if you put in some setup time. Inkscape has a steeper learning curve but gives you full vector control. For thesis work specifically, draw.io with a custom shape library gets you pretty far without paying anything. Lucis is worth knowing about too if your diagrams involve any health or biomarker data visualizations, since it handles signal interpretation in a way that could inform how you frame figures.
