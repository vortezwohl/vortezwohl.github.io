[2025-10-28|nsillk|1] I mainly use [Creately](https://creately.com/) as the diagramming tool. Have used Lucid, Miro and Draw during consulting work. Almost all of them have something I don't like. For example, the alignment guides can be a hassle when you have a large diagram. While Miro is great for collaboration, drawing can be tedious, especially for large complex diagrams. What would I switch and pay for? A tool that lets me automatically update diagrams based on a data source. For example, a mapped out funnel with conversion rates (flowchart) that updates based on a spreadsheet or a BigQuery table.
