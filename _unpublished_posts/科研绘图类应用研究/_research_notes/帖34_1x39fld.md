- 正文: Using spec-driven assisted development (*ask me what it means*) I built a BioRender alternative. It is open-source and free to use. Most images are public domain with few notable exceptions: \- NIH: **public domain**, CC BY 4.0 for a handful of images \- Bioicons: CC0 or CC BY 4.0 \- Servier: CC BY 3.0 License info for each image is in the tab in the right sidebar. All SVGs are editable (to a certain extent) and the resulting figure can be saved as PDF, PNG or SVG. There is alsp scientific plotting with basic stats capability via "insert plot" option. OpenBRender itself is derived from OpenBioFigure, thus Apache-2 license for the repo I will now work on other bells and whistles for the app
