- 正文: Using spec-driven AI-assisted engineering (*vibe-coding*) I built a BioRender alternative. It is open-source and free to use. Most images are public domain with few notable exceptions: \- NIH: **public domain**, CC BY 4.0 for a handful of images \- Bioicons: CC0 or CC BY 4.0 \- Servier: CC BY 3.0 License info for each image is in the tab in the right sidebar. All SVGs are editable (to a certain extent) and the resulting figure can be saved as PDF, PNG or SVG. The app ([DrawPlotia](https://DrawPlotia.gptchatly.com)) itself is derived from OpenBioFigure, thus Apache-2 license for the repo Source: [https://github.com/GPTchatly/DrawPlotia](https://github.com/GPTchatly/DrawPlotia) (FOSS; Apache-2.0) I will now work on introducing a proper plot building and other bells and whistles into the app Edit: Added scientific plotting with basic stats Edit 2: Repo changed name
