- 正文: Currently, BioRender is one of the few softwares that require their website be included as a URL within figure captions. I feel that this is excessive and predatory. Our work should not be a walking advertisement for BioRender. ChimeraX, Pymol, Illustrator, and most other figure building softwares do not require citation in the figure caption but a mention in the methods and a citation. I have an email drafted to address this. What does the community think?
