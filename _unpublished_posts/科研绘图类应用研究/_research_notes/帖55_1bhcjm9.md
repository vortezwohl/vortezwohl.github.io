[2025-05-06|VintageArtist57|1] Its been my profession for over 27 years. I've worked as a scientific illustrator at a large medical research institute for 19 of those years, and my job has changed just recently - I was doing a lot of graphic design as well up til now. My other illustrator friends would always joke about graphic design work being the "necessary evil" you have to do to get to the sci illustration work, if your job is at a university, hospital or research institute. The only scientific illustrators I know who do it exclusively are freelancers.
