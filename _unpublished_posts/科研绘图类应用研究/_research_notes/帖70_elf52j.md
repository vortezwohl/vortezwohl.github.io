[2020-03-06|tizalii|4] I work in the field (have done for 8 years). I mainly work in molecular biology illustration. Learn the business skills of being a freelance illustrator/designer as well as the design skills - they probably won't teach you those on the course. I'm based in the UK rather than the US but I imagine it's fairly similar. Learn some marketing, build a website, talk to your ideal clients about what kind of problems they have and work out which ones you can solve (and be paid for). It also really helps to have a side-gig when you're starting out. I used to do private tutoring.
