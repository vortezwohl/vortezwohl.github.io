# 03 · 用户痛点与工作流全景(科研绘图的真实成本结构)

> 数据源: 帖11/13/14/19/21/22 全量评论 + 帖01-10 交叉; 引用格式 [帖NN|用户名|分数]
> 定位: 本档案回答"科研绘图到底痛在哪"——按痛感强度排序, 全部为用户原话级证据。

## 一、时间黑洞: "做图比做科学还慢"

这是声量最大、跨学科最一致的痛点:

> "Why does making figures take longer than the actual science?"(帖子标题本身) [帖14|r/academia|64分]

> "sounds like a skill issue" → "Hours? Holy smokes I spend days if not weeks making the figures" [帖14|g33ksc13nt1st]

> "Probably hundreds of hours go into every set of figures for a major paper. You spent years on the science why not a small fraction of that to give your results the polish they deserve" [帖14|limpbizkit6]

> "When my students have gathered all the good data to write a paper I tell them congrats, you're now at the halfway point." [帖14|IHTFPhD|9]

> "Why does 'just quickly fixing a figure' never stay quick?" [快照|r/studytips]

时间去哪了(用户自述的碎片): 反复调配色/线型 [帖14|4getprevpassword|44: "two hours of picking the right color gradation, line style"]; 消除留白 [帖14|Efficient_Rhubarb_43]; 对齐/字体/描边 [帖14|limpbizkit6]; 多面板拼版 [帖13|patchwork 讨论]; 从别的软件导来导去 [帖07|xRyuuzetsu]。

## 二、图=论文的门面: 审稿人以图取人

> "There's quite a few papers where you can tell very little work is gone to the figures, which then makes me wonder... if they don't care how the figures look like in a paper, what else the authors didn't care about...? I typically put those in the bin." [帖14|g33ksc13nt1st]

> "A picture says a thousand words. I'd argue that good art can be the difference between reviewers easily understanding your paper from the figures vs them getting frustrated and having to read closer to understand and unfavorably reviewing." [帖02|init2memeit|3]

> "high impact papers uniformly pay attention to the small details... If you submit something with a ton of misaligned figures, haphazard fonts and color schemes, people will ass..." [帖14|limpbizkit6]

反面制衡(同样有力): "realistically it doesn't matter, as long as your schematic is clear and concise. Don't spend a bunch of time on something just because you think it'll help your publication, it won't." [帖02|MysteriousMacrophage|127分]; "I've seen talks with MS Paint-level figures that got the point across better than the slick biorender images everybody else makes." [帖02|stirwise|23]

**结论**: 社区共识是"清晰>华丽", 但现实中评审偏见与顶级期刊审美军备竞赛真实存在 → 用户在两种规范间精神分裂, 这正是"既快又好"工具的情绪缺口。

## 三、审美约束的三重枷锁(无障碍/期刊/传统)

1. **色盲安全**: "try picking things that are colorblind friendly. Colors are great, but if they look the same to someone, it's not helpful. Sometimes that means choosing a different scheme or adding texture rather than color" [帖21|2kgweight|27]; "That is actually much harder than one might think"(色盲种类超出想象) [帖21|The_Anenomy]
2. **黑白打印兼容**: "finding the perfect colour scheme that works for common visual impairments/black and white printing/does not look like total garbage" [帖14|Efficient_Rhubarb_43]
3. **审稿人/PI 个人偏好暴力**: 被批评"(tastefully) colorful graph" [帖21|OP]; 导师只允许灰度 [帖21|Bohrealis]; "my rainbow graph gets shut down everyyy time" [帖21|blueberry_carrie]
4. 期刊技术规格: 300-600 dpi [帖05|toastedbread47]; "always export as SVG or PDF, never PNG, journals will make you redo it otherwise" [帖13|amazngsgr]

## 四、工具碎片化: 每人一个"工具栈"(普查证据)

> "Idk, I've had to learn like four different really complex softwares and all their quirks to push out my *one* project. Sometimes you don't want to have to learn how to be a whiz at 25 UIs to get your paper out" [帖18|Own-Brain9658]

典型组合(原话):
- "matplotlib → SVG → Inkscape 精修" [帖19|Incandescent_Banana|11]
- "R for data visualisation, Inkscape to arrange into figures" [帖11|Eggs76|7]
- "Igor for working up data and making plots. Illustrator for nice figures. Pymol for molecules... export them to illustrator" [帖19|TheLovelyLorelei]
- "RStudio + Adobe Illustrator are all you need" [帖13|TheBurnerAccount420|13]
- "mix of powerpoint, adobe illustrator, biorender and R ggplot" [帖13|lilmissdaydreamin]
- "Excel for almost all of my diagrams and data visualizations. Then there's Origin and MS Paint. The combination of these three can tackle almost everything." [帖13|TsekoD]

**学界的真实现状是"数据工具×示意工具×排版工具"三层拼盘**, 每一层都要钱/学习曲线/导出妥协。缝合这三层 = 最大的产品想象空间。

## 五、一致性/可复现性: 被低估的第二大痛点

> "How does everyone make figures and tables look the exact same?!" [帖14|Meizas]

> "The amount of time I spent on one particular set of figures convinced me to simply create matlab scripts so I could regenerate them quickly and exactly if I needed to relabel anything, change colours/fonts, increase resolution, use different data" [帖11|GoodMerlinpeen|10] — 从手改到代码化的顿悟时刻

> "I made a graph template for a machine that outputs multiple tabs of excel data and it takes seconds to format and pop out a beautiful graph" [帖17|Krispcrap]

> "way faster to redo a figure if most of it is programatically generated vs. having to redo all of the graphical work after the fact if something changes" [帖19|Incandescent_Banana]

驱动因素: 修回改图(审稿意见返工)、换期刊重排版、毕业/离職后失去软件许可 [帖17|deleted|123]、导师换口味 [帖02|stirwise]。**"一次排版, 数据更新全自动同步"** 在评论中被反复描述为理想态。

## 六、技能与教育缺口

- "Doing a graphic design course specifically for scientific figures was one of the best things I did during my PhD. I would recommend it to everyone." [帖14|MisterBreeze]
- "What courses or tutorials are genuinely worth the time" 是 PhD 新生的标准问题 [帖13|OP 完整清单: 哪个软件/数据图与示意图是否分工具/课程/第一年该知道什么]
- 知识以碎片形式流传: PPT 改注册表出 600dpi、ggprism、ggsignif、patchwork、colorbrewer、Servier…… 全靠评论区口口相传, 没有系统性入口
- 机构信号: "I spoke with our research development office... suggesting they hire a full time figure maker. I think there'd be enough interest to keep them fully subscribed." [帖14|Rhawk187|5]
